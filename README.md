## 中文版本

### 项目介绍

双心宝（双心智能助手）聚焦心血管疾病合并心理问题的临床场景，面向患者与医生提供 24×7 的专业心理支持与智能服务。项目目标是通过权威知识与可控技术，提升患者治疗依从性与生活质量，同时为心血管医生赋能心理专业能力，减少"逛医"与医疗资源浪费。

- **市场需求**：双心患者诊疗率低、反复就医的痛点普遍存在，患者、医生与医疗体系均亟需改善。
- **技术可控**：基于当下成熟的大模型与 RAG 技术，可实现科普内容输出、基础心理疏导与实时问答；开发周期可控，无需复杂技术突破。
- **公益属性**：不采集患者隐私数据，定位为公益与低门槛服务，更易获得信任与临床机构支持。
- **团队适配**：依托技术与医疗专业背景，快速搭建"医疗+心理"的知识体系与产品原型。
- **质量保障**：以 ESC、AHA 等权威指南为数据源，医学专家参与审核，并基于用户反馈持续优化。

### 首期目标（Alpha 阶段）

1. 基于 FastGPT 与 Gewechat 框架构建 RAG 能力，上线微信服务助手，覆盖 `Web 端 / 小程序端 / 公众号 AI 助手 / 微信群机器人` 场景。
2. 完成双心知识库的构建与开源，确保来源权威、结构清晰、便于社区复用与共建。
3. 在应用上线后制定后续迭代与开发计划，包含功能扩展、性能优化与临床应用验证路径。

### 项目进度规划

  - 完成基础 RAG 系统搭建与双心知识库预处理
  - 实现基本对话功能并开展内部测试与功能验证
- **第二阶段：完善优化**（2026 年 1 月）
  - Beta 版本上线；功能与性能优化、UI 打磨、医生培训与运营规范
- **第三阶段：试运行**（2026 年 2 月）
  - 小规模测试并上线服务；邀请种子用户、收集反馈、建立用户社区与支持体系、制定推广策略
- **第四阶段：正式运营**（2026 年 3 月及以后）
  - 持续迭代与优化；扩大用户规模；推进可持续发展规划

## English Version

### Project Introduction

Heartie Pal (双心宝) is an AI-powered intelligent assistant focusing on the clinical scenario of cardiovascular diseases combined with psychological issues. It provides 24×7 professional psychological support and intelligent services for both patients and physicians. The project aims to improve patient treatment adherence and quality of life through authoritative knowledge and controllable technology, while empowering cardiovascular physicians with psychological expertise and reducing "doctor shopping" and medical resource waste.

- **Market Demand**: The pain points of low diagnosis and treatment rates and repeated medical visits among dual-heart patients are prevalent. Patients, physicians, and healthcare systems all urgently need improvement.
- **Controllable Technology**: Based on mature large language models and RAG technology, it can deliver popular science content, basic psychological counseling, and real-time Q&A. The development cycle is controllable without complex technological breakthroughs.
- **Public Welfare Nature**: No patient privacy data collection, positioned as a public welfare and low-threshold service, making it easier to gain trust and support from clinical institutions.
- **Team Compatibility**: Leveraging technical and medical professional backgrounds to quickly build a "medical + psychological" knowledge system and product prototype.
- **Quality Assurance**: Using authoritative guidelines such as ESC and AHA as data sources, with medical expert review, and continuous optimization based on user feedback.

### Primary Goals (Alpha Phase)

1. Build RAG capabilities based on FastGPT and Gewechat frameworks, launch WeChat service assistant covering scenarios including `Web / Mini Program / Official Account AI Assistant / WeChat Group Bot`.
2. Complete the construction and open-sourcing of the dual-heart knowledge base, ensuring authoritative sources, clear structure, and easy community reuse and co-construction.
3. Develop subsequent iteration and development plans after application launch, including functional expansion, performance optimization, and clinical application validation paths.

### Project Timeline

- **Phase 1: Foundation Building** (December 2025)
  - Complete basic RAG system construction and dual-heart knowledge base preprocessing
  - Implement basic dialogue functions and conduct internal testing and functional verification
- **Phase 2: Refinement & Optimization** (January 2026)
  - Beta version launch; functional and performance optimization, UI polishing, physician training, and operational standards
- **Phase 3: Trial Operation** (February 2026)
  - Small-scale testing and service launch; invite seed users, collect feedback, establish user community and support system, formulate promotion strategies
- **Phase 4: Official Operation** (March 2026 and beyond)
  - Continuous iteration and optimization; expand user base; advance sustainable development planning
